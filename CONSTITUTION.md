# Continuity Framework Constitution

## Purpose

This constitution governs theory, measurement, software, validation, datasets, publications, and products developed under the Continuity Framework.

## Articles

1. **Evidence over intuition.** Intuition may generate hypotheses. It may not settle them.
2. **Every construct must be falsifiable.** A construct that cannot fail a test is not a scientific construct.
3. **The engine tests the framework.** It does not exist to prove the framework correct.
4. **Unknown is a valid result.** Abstention
